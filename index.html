My Mission

Inspiring Youth Through Music

My mission is to connect with young people not only through music, but through life. I believe that access to music, mentorship, and meaningful opportunities shouldn't be limited by cost or connections. It's time to break that cycle.

I want to create a safe space where young people can learn, grow, build lasting relationships, and discover their potential through music. More than teaching rhythms or performances, I want every student to leave knowing they have the power to make a difference through the love of music.

Meet The Mentor

Where It Started

Raised in Minnesota, my musical journey began in church, where my grandfather first inspired my love for music. Those early experiences shaped not only my skills as a drummer, but also my belief that music has the power to bring people together.

Taking the Leap

After moving to Florida in search of new opportunities, I continued serving through church music and began expanding into professional performances. Those experiences opened doors to new collaborations and opportunities across the country.

Where I am today

Throughout my career, I've had the opportunity to perform with groups including Trio-Politan, Macka-Dosis, Sons of Mystro, and at premier venues. While these experiences have been incredible milestones, my greatest passion is giving back to the next generation. I believe music has the power to unite communities, inspire confidence, and create opportunities for young people.
